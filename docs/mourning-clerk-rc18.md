# The Mourning Clerk · RC18

The new office enemy is a female limestone figure with tear tracks, a carved braid, numbered archive clothes and asymmetric stone wings. The right wing remains full. Fractured plates and exposed bronze repairs mark the other wing. When she notices a sustained stare she lifts her head, then lunges into the first-person camera on capture. Classic has its own hand-drawn sprite and response animation.

## Read and listen

Press E at the Record 17 phonograph near the office lift. It plays a short warning in English or Mandarin, depending on language. The journal keeps the written instructions. The spoken warning, live gaze bar and post-capture hint give different pieces of information. The Classic control to look down is C, with an equivalent touch button. In 3D the player can tilt the mouse view toward the floor, look to one side or break eye contact behind a wall.

Both editions have contextual voice cues in the boiler room, office and roof. The new directions use stock localized synthetic voices. The archive's existing human dialogue is retained, and the clerk's sob comes from an independently licensed human recording. The mixing removes delayed copies of speech. The gramophone retains a filtered tone and needle sound. The directional sob continues to come from the creature.
